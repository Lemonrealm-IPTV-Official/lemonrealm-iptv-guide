## What is LemonRealm?

**LemonRealm** is an IPTV streaming service for live TV, movies and series. It works on smart TVs, streaming boxes (like the onn. 4K Google TV box), iPhone, iPad, Android phones and PC.

After you sign up on [lemonrealm.com](https://lemonrealm.com/), LemonRealm emails you your **Xtream Codes** (username, password, server URL) or an **M3U playlist link**. You add those details to an IPTV player app once, and you are ready to watch.

## How to log in to LemonRealm

There is no website login. Your LemonRealm login lives inside your IPTV player app.

1. Open the email from LemonRealm with your Xtream Codes (username, password, server) or M3U link.
2. Install an IPTV player app on your device (see the picks below).
3. In the app, choose **Xtream Codes login** and paste your details, or add your **M3U link**.
4. Save, wait for the channels to load, and start watching.

Lost your details? Check your Spam folder, then see the [LemonRealm login help page](https://lemonrealm.com/login/).
